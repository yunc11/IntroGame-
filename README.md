# Dimension Instability

## About the Game
Dimension Instability is a 3D survival game made in Unity for our ECS657U coursework.

The aim is to collect three energy cores while avoiding enemies around the map. Once you've collected all three, you need to reach the portal to escape.

## How to Play
- **WASD** - Move around
- **Mouse** - Look around and control the camera.
- **E** - Enter the portal (Only when you have 3 energy cores)
- Collect all three energy cores around the map.
- Escape from enemies while collecting the cores.
- Reach the portal to win.

## Features
- Player movement and camera controls
- Health and damage system
- Three collectible energy cores
- Three enemies around the map
- Escape portal
- Win screen after escaping

## How to Run the Game
1. Open the project in Unity 2023.1.22f1.
2. Open `Assets/Scenes/MainScene.unity`.
3. Press Play to start the game.
